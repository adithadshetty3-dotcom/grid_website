FAQ

We are the official technical club of K. S. School of Engineering and Management. We are a club run by students, for students. Our focus is on bridging the gap between academics and real-world applications.

  • • GRID events are open to all students who are eager to learn, compete, and collaborate.
  • • Students from all the departments are welcome.

You can join GRID by applying during the recruitment phase, which is announced on our website and social media. Stay updated on announcements and selection criteria to become a part of our team!

Participating in GRID events helps you enhance your technical and soft skills, gain hands-on experience, network with like-minded peers and industry professionals, and even win exciting prizes or recognition!

GRID organizes a variety of events, including coding competitions, ideation challenges, workshops, guest lectures, networking sessions, and non-technical events to enhance creativity and teamwork.
